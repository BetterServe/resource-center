What happens next

Watch for a text from BABY PASS

The MOMS Helpline has sent your enrollment. In a few minutes you will get a text from BABY PASS asking you to reply YES to confirm your phone number. Reply YES to that text. Nothing moves forward until you do.

Texts come from

463-837-1840

After you reply YES, the text you get back tells you when your card will arrive. Your BABY PASS card comes in its own text from that number, with a link to open it. The card opens on your phone, so there is nothing to download and nothing comes in the mail.

Save 463-837-1840 in your contacts as BABY PASS. It is not spam. Every message about your benefit comes from this number, and it is also the number to text or call when you need help.

How much, and for how long

What BABY PASS gives your family

There are two benefits. Your family may get one or both, depending on what you applied for.

Diapers

$40

per child, every month, for 6 months

1 child: $402 children: $803 or more: $120
  • Up to 3 children
  • A new balance arrives every 31 days

Car seat

$100

one time, toward a new car seat

  • Use it within 90 days
  • One per family
  • If the seat costs more than $100, you pay the rest

Diaper money does not carry over. Each monthly balance has to be used within 31 days. Whatever is left after that is removed, and the next month's balance takes its place. Your card shows the last day to use what you have.

31 days is not the same as a month. If your first balance arrives on February 1, your next one comes around March 3, not March 1. Go by the date on your card, not the calendar.

What you can buy

Diapers, diapering supplies, and car seats

With the diaper benefit

Diapers and diapering supplies on the BABY PASS approved list. Name brands and store brands both count.

With the car seat benefit

You can buy

  • Rear-facing infant car seats
  • Convertible car seats
  • All-in-one or 3-in-1 car seats that start rear-facing

You cannot buy

  • Booster seats of any kind
  • Used, refurbished, or open-box seats
  • Car seat and stroller sets
  • Add-ons like mirrors, head supports, or seat protectors

BABY PASS pays only for covered items, up to your balance. You pay for anything else in your cart, and for any amount over your balance, with another payment method. This is true for diapers and car seats, in the store and online.

After you buy a car seat

Get it checked for free

How a seat is installed matters as much as which seat you buy. Indiana has free car seat inspection stations in every part of the state. A certified technician will check that your seat fits your child and your car, and show you how to install it.

Find an inspection station near you. Call ahead to make an appointment.

Cuánto y por cuánto tiempo

Lo que BABY PASS le da a su familia

Hay dos beneficios. Su familia puede recibir uno o los dos, según lo que haya solicitado.

Pañales

por niño, cada mes, durante 6 meses

1 niño: $402 niños: $803 o más: $120

Asiento de seguridad para el auto

$100

una sola vez, para un asiento nuevo

El dinero para pañales no se acumula. Cada saldo mensual se tiene que usar dentro de 31 días. Lo que quede después se borra y llega el saldo del mes siguiente. Su tarjeta muestra el último día para usar lo que tiene.

31 días no es lo mismo que un mes. Si su primer saldo llega el 1 de febrero, el siguiente llegará alrededor del 3 de marzo, no el 1 de marzo. Guíese por la fecha de su tarjeta, no por el calendario.

Qué puede comprar

Pañales, artículos para cambiar pañales y asientos para el auto

Con el beneficio de pañales

Pañales y artículos para cambiar pañales que estén en la lista aprobada de BABY PASS. Sirven tanto las marcas conocidas como las marcas de la tienda.

Con el beneficio de asiento para el auto

Sí puede comprar

  • Asientos para bebé orientados hacia atrás
  • Asientos convertibles
  • Asientos todo en uno o 3 en 1 que empiezan orientados hacia atrás

No puede comprar

  • Asientos usados, reacondicionados o de caja abierta

BABY PASS solo paga los artículos cubiertos, hasta su saldo. Usted paga con otra forma de pago cualquier otra cosa en su carrito y cualquier cantidad que pase de su saldo. Esto aplica a los pañales y a los asientos para el auto, en la tienda y en línea.
